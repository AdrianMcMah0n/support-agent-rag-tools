# Payments

## Accepted methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Larkspur Home gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery or bank transfers.

## When you are charged
Your card is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## Pay in instalments
Orders over $200 can be split into 4 interest-free payments through PayPal Pay in 4.
