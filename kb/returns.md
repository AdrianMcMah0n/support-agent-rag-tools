# Returns and refunds

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Items that cannot be returned
Personalised items, opened candles and gift cards cannot be returned.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return reaching our warehouse. Return shipping is free if the item arrived damaged or was wrong; otherwise a $6 return label fee is deducted from the refund.
