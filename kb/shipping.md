# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery within the United States takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

## Shipping costs
Standard delivery is free on orders of $50 or more. Below $50, standard delivery costs $5.95. Express delivery costs $14.95 on any order.

## Carriers and tracking
We ship with UPS and USPS. A tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse, and it also appears in the order details.
