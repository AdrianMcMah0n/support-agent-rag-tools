# Warranty

## What is covered
Furniture and lighting carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Textiles and decor carry a 1-year warranty.

## What is not covered
Normal wear, accidental damage and damage from incorrect assembly are not covered.

## Making a claim
Send the order number and a photo of the defect. Approved claims receive a replacement or, if the item is no longer sold, a full refund.
